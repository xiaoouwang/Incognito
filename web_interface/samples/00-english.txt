Marie Dupont, aged 43, lives at 15 Rue des Orangers in Nice. She has been working as a teacher at Lycée Masséna since September 2018. Her husband, Pierre Martin, is a doctor at Pasteur Hospital. They have two children, Lucas, aged 12, and Emma, aged 8.

Marie was born on March 14, 1983, in Lyon. Her email address is marie.dupont@example.com, and she can be reached by phone at 06 12 34 56 78.

During an interview conducted on October 5, 2026, Marie explains that she recently moved to the Cimiez neighborhood. She mentions that her husband often works night shifts and that their son Lucas attends the same school where she teaches.

A few minutes later, she adds: “My father, Jean Dupont, was a professor at Université Côte d’Azur. He retired in 2020. My sister Sophie now works at Cannes City Hall.”

Finally, Marie states that she is the only Chinese-language teacher at her school who participated in a university exchange program with Beijing in 2019.
